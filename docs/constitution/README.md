# The Continuity Constitution

The Constitution is the project’s founding specification for durable authority, relationships, obligations and succession. It describes the intended rules and boundaries; it is not a claim that every ambition has been implemented or that software can determine legal responsibility.

Read the [complete governing text](CONTINUITY-Constitution-v0.1-as-amended-001-002-003-004-golden.txt). It is preserved byte-for-byte, not rewritten for this release.

## In plain language

- Keep who an agent is, what is running, what it may do and what it owes separate.
- Make authority explicit and bounded; delegation and replacement must not silently broaden it.
- Record why an action was allowed, denied or could not be decided from the available evidence.
- Preserve surviving responsibilities and unresolved outcomes when execution ends or changes.
- State the history, trust, disclosure and deployment assumptions behind every answer.

These bullets are a non-governing introduction. The full text, including its amendments and precedence, controls. Core 0.2.2 is a bounded local reference implementation; public-chain durability and wider institutional aspirations do not become proven merely because they appear in the Constitution.
