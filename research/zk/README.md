# Historical ZK checks — completed finite experiment

The accepted engineering snapshot contains eight named r3/r3.1 proof chains: Count, cumulative budget, handover in Both and State modes, three-period carry, revocation, full100 Budget, and signed request-headroom exhaustion. All four producer phases succeeded for each case, followed by final native P verification. [Recorded results and commitments](RESULTS.json) distinguish the seven r3 programs from the corrected r3.1 addition. These counts are not a new total of all historical scenarios.

The source folders publish the final guest, native derivation, proof host, proof loader and quantitative verifier. Original-file and published-file hashes are recorded in [source provenance](../../PUBLIC-SOURCE-PROVENANCE.json). Environment-bound paths in source harnesses are explicit placeholders. Keys/SRS, private input/proof bodies, runtime/configuration snapshots, loader dependencies and vendored toolchains are not bundled. This is inspectable experimental source, not a turnkey clean-machine proof runner; the published path adaptations have no new proof or guest-build credit. Source review and recorded results apply to their pinned original editions.

The signed exhaustion case admits an amount of 3 once, then refuses the next request for3 without another admission, charge or dispatch. Its quantitative checks were performed separately. Literal Q cryptographic verification inside P was omitted; guest quantitativeQVerified and general component policyCompositionVerified remain false. Recorded owner durations are about 7.32 hours application, 2.43 hours aggregation, 1.60 minutes static and 1.59 minutes wrapping. Host/owner measurements are distinct; CPU seconds are unavailable. The finite roadmap was accepted and closed.
